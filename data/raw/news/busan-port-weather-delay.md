A fictional winter storm delayed cargo departures from Busan Port by up to six
hours on Tuesday. Terminal operators temporarily reduced crane activity while
wind speeds were above the site safety threshold.

Nova Logistics said the backlog should clear by Friday if the forecast improves.
The company advised customers with temperature-sensitive cargo to confirm revised
gate times before arriving at the terminal.
