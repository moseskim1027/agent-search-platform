Han River Metro announced a synthetic late-night service expansion on the Blue
Line. Beginning in October, two additional trains will depart after midnight on
Friday and Saturday evenings.

The fictional operator said the change responds to rising weekend demand around
the Digital Media City and Riverside Park stations. The timetable also includes
an extra transfer window to the Green Line at Central Station.

Passengers can check the planned arrival time in the fictional MetroNow app.
The operator will review crowding and wait-time data after the first eight weeks.
